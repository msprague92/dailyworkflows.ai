Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Workflow AI Solutions LLC ("Workflow AI", "we"), 8692 Windsor Hill Boulevard, North Charleston, SC 29420.

The short version

Workflow AI is software that runs on your dealership's own computer. Your inventory, the listings it writes and the photos it saves stay on that computer. Our server sees only what it needs to keep your license working. We do not sell data and we do not run advertising.

What we collect, and why

  • At checkout (handled by Stripe): the dealership's name and website, the buyer's name and email, and payment details. Stripe processes the payment; we never see card numbers. We keep the dealership name, website and contact email to issue the license and to support you.
  • From the software, each night: your license code, the software version, the number of vehicles listed, and the time of the check-in. This keeps the license active, tells us if an installation has stopped running, and lets us see when a lot has outgrown its plan.
  • When you write to us: your email and whatever you send to support@dailyworkflows.ai.
  • On this website: standard web-server logs (IP address, pages requested, browser type) kept by our hosting provider, GitHub Pages. We do not set tracking cookies or use analytics scripts.

What stays on your computer

Everything about your vehicles: the pages read from your website, the listing text, the photos, the posting plan, the nightly logs and the status page. None of it is uploaded to us. If you cancel, you can simply delete the folder.

Who we share with

  • Stripe — payments, invoices and your billing portal. Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Supabase — hosts our license server and its small database (license codes, check-ins). Data is stored in the United States.
  • Microsoft 365 — our email.

We share data with no one else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

License and check-in records are kept while your subscription is active and for 12 months after it ends, then deleted. Billing records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. Support emails are kept for two years.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what we hold about your dealership, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it once your subscription has ended. Write to support@dailyworkflows.ai. You can cancel a subscription from the Stripe billing portal linked in your receipts.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will post the new version here and email active subscribers before it takes effect.
