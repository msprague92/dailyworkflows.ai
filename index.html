How it works

It installs on one computer at the dealership and runs by itself overnight. Nothing to type in, no spreadsheets to keep up with.

1

Reads your website

Each night it pulls every vehicle your site shows for sale — year, make, model, trim, price, mileage, VIN and photos — straight from the pages your customers see.

2

Prepares the listings

One listing per vehicle, written from the facts on your site: a clean headline, miles and price, your standing statements, the VIN and your sign-off. Photos are saved alongside, in order.

3

Builds the day's plan

What to post, what to renew and what to mark sold — based on what changed on your website. Your posting tool or your team works from that plan each morning.

What you get

  • A Windows program installed in about ten minutes, with a license code from your subscription.
  • A nightly run at the time you choose; the PC wakes up for it.
  • Listings written only from facts stated on your website — nothing invented.
  • A status page that tells you, in plain words, whether last night worked and why any vehicle was held back.
  • Automatic updates.

What we don't do

  • We don't post to Facebook for you and we never touch your Facebook account. Posting is done by your team or by a posting tool of your choice, working from the plan Workflow AI prepares.
  • We don't change your website. If a vehicle page is missing a price, mileage or VIN, that vehicle is held and the status page tells you.
  • We don't keep your inventory in the cloud — it stays on your computer.

Questions

Will it work with my website?

Most dealer websites, yes — including sites built on Carsforsale.com, Dealer Car Search, DealerOn and similar platforms. Before you install, we check your site and tell you exactly what it can and cannot read. Sites that block automated visitors cannot be read.

What does a listing look like?

Year make model and trim, then miles and price, your standing statements (for example clean title, recently serviced, passed inspection), the VIN and your dealership's sign-off — the same for every vehicle, so your listings look consistent.

What happens if my inventory grows past my plan?

Up to 10% over, nothing changes — you'll see a note on the status page and we'll reach out about the next plan. Beyond that, the plan keeps the vehicles it covers and holds the rest until the plan is upgraded. Nothing already listed is pulled.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from your billing portal or by emailing us. The software keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for.

Where does my data go?

Your inventory, listings and plans stay on your computer. Our server only sees your license code, the dealership name and website, how many vehicles are listed, and the time of each nightly check-in.
